Chief Counsel

Priya Raghavan

Writes down what the family has decided, before it has to be argued.

Priya leads the legal function of the office: the structures the family holds assets through, the documents that govern succession, and the standing review that keeps both current as law and circumstance move.

She practised for over a decade in private client and trusts work at an international firm before moving in-house, and has spent most of her career on the same problem from two sides — advising families on structure, then living with the structures that advice produced.

What she handles

Trust and holding-entity structuring, shareholder and family governance agreements, wills and succession documents, philanthropic vehicles, and the family's charter and delegated authorities. She instructs and manages outside counsel in every jurisdiction the family touches, and keeps that panel short.

On the compliance side she owns regulatory filings, beneficial ownership registers, sanctions and AML screening for counterparties, data protection, and the office's conflicts and confidentiality policies.

How she works

Priya's first question about any proposal is what it looks like in fifteen years, under a different tax regime, with a different generation reading it. Documents are drafted to be understood by the people who inherit them, not only by the lawyers who wrote them.

Nothing on this page is legal advice, and correspondence through this site does not create a solicitor–client or attorney–client relationship.
