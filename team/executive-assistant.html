Executive Assistant

Anneke de Vries

Keeps the office running so nothing reaches the family half-finished.

Anneke is the operating centre of the office. Every request that arrives — a board date to be moved, a flight to be rebooked, a contractor to be vetted, a document to be found and produced — passes through her, and most of them are closed before anyone else hears about them.

She joined Kroonenburg after more than a decade supporting principals in private banking and a listed industrials group, where she built the habit that defines the role here: assume the request has three dependencies nobody has mentioned yet, and resolve those first.

What she handles

Anneke owns the family's calendar across time zones, coordinates travel and household logistics, manages vendor and property relationships, and maintains the office's document custody — what is held, where, who may see it, and how long it is kept.

She also runs the intake side of the office. Enquiries submitted through this site reach her first; she routes them to the CFO or Chief Counsel where they belong and answers the rest herself, usually the same day.

Working with her

Anneke prefers a single message with the whole picture over a chain of fragments — a date range rather than a date, the constraint behind the request, and who else is involved. Given that, the answer is almost always a confirmation rather than a question.
