Kroonenburg Family Office is a single-family office built around one mandate: protect what has been earned, grow it with discipline, and hand it on intact. A small, senior team handles the capital, the counsel and the calendar so the family can focus on the decisions that matter.

What the office does

Three functions, one table.

Family capital rarely fails for want of a good idea. It fails on administration, on tax and structure, on succession left unwritten. The office is organised to close those gaps.

01

Capital & reporting

Consolidated reporting across entities and custodians, treasury and liquidity planning, manager selection and oversight, and an annual review that treats the balance sheet as a whole.

02

Governance & counsel

Trust and entity structuring, succession and estate documents, philanthropic vehicles, and the standing legal review that keeps cross-border holdings compliant and current.

03

Household & operations

Diary and travel, vendor and property coordination, document custody and confidentiality, and a single point of contact for every request that reaches the family.

How we work

Quiet, documented, unhurried.

Discretion by default

Nothing about the family's affairs leaves the office. Access is least-privilege, records are retained deliberately, and the team is small on purpose.

One consolidated view

Every entity, account and obligation rolls into a single quarterly picture — so decisions are made against the whole, not a slice.

Decisions written down

Mandates, delegations and rationale are recorded. The next generation inherits the reasoning, not just the assets.

Start a conversation.

Enquiries from family members, advisers, counterparties and prospective partners are read by the office and answered directly.
